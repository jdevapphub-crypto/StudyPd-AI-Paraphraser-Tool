How to Paraphrase University Assignments

A practical guide for UNISA, UJ, TUT, and other SA students.

Paraphrasing is one of the most important skills you will learn at university. It is also one of the most commonly misunderstood.

What paraphrasing actually means

Restating someone else's idea in your own words while keeping the original meaning. It is different from summarising and different from quoting.

Step 1 — Read until you understand

Read the original passage twice. Close the source. Then say the idea out loud in your own words.

🎓 Research Guide for South African Students

Everything you need to do research properly at a South African university.

1. Choose and narrow your topic

  • Pick a topic you can finish in your time and budget.
  • Turn it into one clear research question.
  • Write 2 to 3 objectives.

2. Research proposal checklist

  • Title, background and problem statement
  • Research question, aim and objectives
  • Literature review outline
  • Methodology

3. Ethics clearance

  • Apply to your faculty REC before collecting data.
  • Informed consent and information sheet.
  • Follow POPIA.

4. Where to find sources

  • EBSCOhost, ProQuest, JSTOR, Sabinet
  • Google Scholar and SciELO South Africa
  • Stats SA, HSRC, CSIR reports
